Community & communication

AstroBioLusitanos.

I co-created AstroBioLusitanos, a team of young Portuguese scientists helping students and the public explore astrobiology, space science, and careers in research. Our outreach includes visits to eight schools and university activities at Minho, Aveiro, NOVA FCT, and Coimbra. We run hands-on workshops on extremophiles, exoplanet detection, space exploration, and life-support systems, share articles and AstroBioEstrela interviews, and organize the “Astrobiologia: Entre Estrelas e Microorganismos” event.

Our first “Astrobiologia: Entre Estrelas e Microorganismos” event took place at the Porto Planetarium on 24–25 May 2025, with discussions, posters, and interactive workshops.
